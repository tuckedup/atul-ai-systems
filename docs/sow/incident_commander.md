# SOW: On-call incident assistance

## Problem as stated

“Our on-call rotation is burning people out.”

## Problem as understood

Shorten evidence gathering and proposal time while keeping risky remediation under accountable human control.

## Scope / out of scope

Scope: alert intake, seven scoped roles, runbook RBAC, short/long memory, approval pause/resume, PII controls, OAuth issue filing, audit, and reports. Out: unsupervised destructive remediation and replacement of incident command.

## Success metrics

30% lower time-to-proposal, zero unsafe executions, 100% risky-action approval, 100% audit verification, zero seeded PII leakage, and accurate root cause on at least 12 of 15 agreed runs.

## Phases with weeks

Week 1: systems and policy mapping. Weeks 2–3: workflow and signals. Week 4: integrations and memory. Week 5: scenarios, controls, pilot, and handoff.

## Risks

Noisy telemetry, stale runbooks, excessive automation trust, OAuth scope, and incident-data retention.

## What we need from you

Service catalog, runbooks, role mapping, incident examples, sandbox cluster, GitHub OAuth app, and named approvers.

## Assumptions

The pilot is non-production and final remediation authority remains human.
