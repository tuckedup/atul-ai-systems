## 1. Closure

Majority voting over five v18 calls (A1) **corrected 0 errors and introduced 2 (dev) / 3 (TRAIN)**; its kappa (dev 0.6403, TRAIN 0.3999) is below A0 (0.6745 / 0.4476) and also below the mean of the five single runs (0.6576 / 0.4160). ## 2. What distinct signal could the learned classifier exploit? (answer: none measurable on existing data)
| Question | Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Can anything built from the five v18 verdicts help? | Of A0's 54 pooled errors, **44 (81%) occur where all five verdicts agree** (dev 15 of 19, TRAIN 29 of 35). Only 10 errors lie in the 23 disagreeing cases, and voting fixed none of them | Variance reduction is capped at 10 errors even if an oracle picked the right verdict in each disagreeing case; A1 captured 0 |
| Do v18's soft scores carry extra information where the errors are? | 38 of the 45 false passes sit in the **169 unanimous-pass cases, where the raw score is exactly 1.0 for all five calls**: zero variation, so a learned model cannot separate them using any v18 score feature. | Do structural features help? | Within the unanimous-pass cases: sentence length AUC 0.59 [0.47, 0.70], source length 0.46 [0.31, 0.58], digit count 0.60 [0.48, 0.69], negation/modal 0.58 [0.50, 0.66] | Weak; intervals include 0.5 |

Within unanimous-reject (only 6 supported): 0.90 [0.80, 0.97] | The only measurable complementary signal, but it is the signal the earlier MiniCheck + v18 combiner already used, and that combiner scored dev kappa 0.593 vs v18 0.6745 (interval [-0.214, +0.015]) |

## 3. Training-to-evaluation policy differences (what A2 would learn on versus be screened on)
| Dimension | RAGTruth fit sample | RouteBench TRAIN + dev | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supported share | 93.2% in the population (6.8% unsupported); the drawn sample is 67.8% supported by design | 56.8% overall; dev 61.7%, TRAIN 51.9%; MediaS 45.2%, MeetB 61.8%, AggreFact-CNN 72.4% | Class-balanced weights and class-conditional rates are meant to neutralise prevalence, but the unsupported class is rare and different in RAGTruth |
| Label provenance | Sentence labelled unsupported if it overlaps an annotated hallucination span (45 boundary-crossing sentences dropped); the manifest records these are not independent expert sentence annotations | Sentence-level factual-consistency labels with upstream rater disagreement (TofuEval) and aggregated labels (AggreFact) | The same sentence can be judged differently under the two policies |
| Domain and generator | LLM summaries of CNN/DM news articles | Dialogue transcripts (MediaSum, MeetingBank) plus CNN/DM, from other summarisation systems | Error mix differs: v18 here is false-pass heavy (45 FP / 9 FR); on fresh documents it was false-rejection heavy (37 FR / 5 FP) |
| Lengths | median sentence 128 chars, source 4,065 | median 141 / 4,367 (AggreFact-CNN sentences median 296) | Moderate mismatch |
| Rights | CNN/DM text is a private-research risk acceptance only | Terms forbid training; evaluation-only here | Unchanged from earlier documents |

**Band-transfer proxy (MiniCheck only, using the earlier pilot's scores on 1,998 RAGTruth rows, 147 unsupported):** bands set for a 10% false-pass and 10% false-reject rate on RAGTruth (pass at >= 0.970, reject at <= 0.50) realised on RouteBench dev / TRAIN: false passes 10.2% / 12.9%, false rejections 7.6% / 6.0%, confident coverage 51% / 57% (RAGTruth itself: 9.5%, 7.7%, 60%). So class-conditional bands transferred reasonably **for this one feature**. The 0.5 point does not transfer: 88.9% of RAGTruth rows score at least 0.5 against 72% of RouteBench rows. AUC was similar (0.84 vs 0.80). This says nothing about how v18 behaves on RAGTruth, which was never observed.

## 4. Evidence for another paid experiment
Weak. For: (i) the band-transfer proxy above; (ii) MiniCheck's within-pass AUC of 0.69 shows some complementary signal for false passes; (iii) a 1,050-row fit is less prone to overfit than a 129-row fit. Against: (i) 81% of A0's errors are invisible to anything computed from the five v18 verdicts; (ii) the three v18 features are constant on the false-pass mass; (iii) the only complementary signal was already tried and failed; (iv) the screening gate would be passed by a useless candidate roughly 10-14% of the time yet by a true +0.03 gain only about 58-72% of the time, so a pass or a fail would be hard to read; (v) it costs about $42 plus a GPU job whose feature equivalence is unproven.

## 5. One bounded recommendation
**Do not run Stage 1b or any further paid judge experiment now. Run no-API, no-GPU human adjudication of the residual errors first, then decide.**
- *What:* independent, blind adjudication (annotators see the source and the sentence under the written policy; no labels, model verdicts or hypothesis) of the **44 cases on which all five v18 calls are wrong** (38 false passes + 6 false rejections), mixed in an unlabelled order with 44 randomly drawn unanimous-correct cases as a control. Original labels and splits stay untouched; results go in a separately named file.
- *Decision rule, to be frozen before annotation:* if independent adjudication sides with v18 or marks uncertain for >= 50% of the 44, the original-label target is label- or policy-limited: stop judge work against the original benchmark and treat further effort as a new benchmark. If it sides with the original label for >= 75%, the residual errors are genuine judge leniency and a targeted paid experiment on that failure mode (starting from the 38 false passes, not from generic features) is justified. Anything in between: stop, no paid run.
- *Cost:* 88 cases x 2 annotators, plus adjudication of disagreements; unpriced and I will not guess; no API, GPU or download.
- *Uncertainties:* (1) 44 cases, and only 6 false rejections, give wide intervals; (2) all documents are already inspected, so this characterises residual errors and does not prove a method; (3) annotators' own agreement may be low, in which case the outcome is "unresolvable", not "label-limited"; (4) TofuEval transcripts are CC-BY-NC-SA, so sharing them with a paid vendor needs a rights check, while in-house annotation does not; (5) the AI-assisted cause audit of the earlier fresh-document set (57% policy/precision conflicts, 27% judge errors, 16% ambiguous, provisional) came from a different error profile and cannot be transferred to these false passes; (6) the thresholds in the decision rule are my proposal and need your approval; (7) nothing here shows 0.74 is unreachable.
